JANSAHAY — Government Scheme Eligibility Checker

JANSAHAY is a project concept focused on making government welfare schemes easier to discover and understand. The goal is to help people explore relevant schemes and understand eligibility requirements through a simple, accessible interface.

VOLTCARE — Electricity Fault Reporting System

VOLTCARE is a project concept designed to make electricity fault reporting and complaint tracking more convenient. The proposed experience includes fault reporting, photo uploads, location details, complaint tracking, and status updates.

Focus areas: User experience, complaint management, and service accessibility.

SAFEHER — Personal Safety Concept

SAFEHER is a concept focused on personal safety and making useful safety features easier to access through a digital interface.

Focus areas: User-centred design, accessibility, and safety-focused product planning.

KAAMSETU — Local Skills and Jobs Platform

KAAMSETU is a project concept aimed at connecting people who offer local services with individuals looking for those skills. The idea focuses on making local work and service discovery more accessible.

DSA Quest — Gamified Learning Platform

DSA Quest is an idea for making Data Structures and Algorithms easier to learn through interactive challenges, progress-based learning, and game-inspired experiences.

The goal is to help students practise problem-solving in a more engaging and approachable way.

Focus areas: Learning experience, product decisions, user experience, and MVP planning.
